Repair, not replace.

How we work on old buildings

An old house is built in layers: plaster, trim, hardware and a century of paint. We keep what is original, mend what is tired, and add the next layer with traditional techniques and the same care.

From paint and paper to full-scale restoration.

One crew for the whole house, from a single papered wall to a heritage facade.

Interior painting
  1. Plaster, trim, ceilings, heights

    Interior painting

    Razor-sharp cut lines and the fine detail of century interiors, from tall stairwells to plaster ceilings.

  2. Doors, porches, siding, trim

    Exterior painting

    Doors, porches and trim prepared properly, then painted in colours that suit the age of the house.

  3. Paperhanging and removal

    Wallpaper

    Bold patterns matched at every seam. Our paperhanger has been hanging paper for more than thirty years.

  4. Heritage carpentry

    Carpentry & restoration

    Honest repair with traditional building techniques, often with reclaimed lumber, so original work stays in place.

  5. Murals, antiquing, stained glass

    Decorative art & murals

    Murals, antiqued finishes, nouveau detail and stained glass, from a decorative artist who is always ready to pull a rabbit out of the hat.

  6. Cabinets, built-ins, doors

    Spray finishing

    Our in-house spray finisher gives cabinets, built-ins and doors a smooth, even finish.
